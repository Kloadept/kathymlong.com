Long

Kathy M. Long is the founder and CEO of NixIt AI, an SMS-first support platform that helps neurodivergent people manage daily life by text message and connects them with their family circle and their coach. She started NixIt for her son Jack, who is autistic, and for a house full of brains that run a different OS, after three decades of operating experience convinced her that the help on offer hovered too much and protected too little.

Kathy's career began in telecom in Chicago, selling secure networks and data center infrastructure to the financial sector. In Colorado she stepped away to raise her first child, founded and ran a cloth diaper business, went back to school for nursing and spent a year at a children's hospital, then returned to telecom and rose into leadership before moving into absence management, supporting employers and their attorneys on leave and benefits programs. She spent the last stretch before NixIt in startups, running support, revenue operations, and eventually operations as a whole, and created the Green-to-Growth Framework, a method for turning churn data into growth intelligence.

Kathy was born in Akron, Ohio, was the first in her family to go to college, and played soccer at The University of Toledo before starting her career in Chicago. She now lives in Broomfield, Colorado, outside Boulder. She holds a master's degree in organizational leadership and a bachelor's degree in communication and public relations, and serves on the advisory board of Women in AI Colorado. She speaks on AI for neurodivergent lives, founding in disability tech, and running a company and a family of six at the same time.
